03 · WHO DOES WHAT

I would keep policy and judgment inside OpenAI.

One issue record should follow the customer across self-service, automation, a partner, and an internal specialist. That record needs the issue type, prior attempts, risk level, handoffs, and final outcome.

Product help and AI

Handle tested, low-risk requests with a clear route to a person when the answer fails or the issue carries risk.

Partner teams

Handle repeatable cases in trained queues, document failures, and send patterns back to the knowledge and product owners.

Internal specialists

Own sensitive cases, unclear policy, product defects, incidents, and the final decision on escalations.

OpenAI would own

Routing rules, knowledge, AI release gates, forecasts, quality calibration, partner contracts, and Product and Engineering priorities.

Partners would own

Staffing, coaching, and execution in approved queues, with audited outcomes and a tested backup route for peaks.

Expected effect and tradeoff

I expect better product help to reduce repeatable demand, partner coaching to improve durable resolution, and trained flex capacity to limit backlog swings. I would accept slower automation expansion and some reserve cost until the customer outcomes support more volume.

I would review service and staffing weekly, calibrate quality across sites monthly, and revisit contracts quarterly. I would use a base capacity commitment with a reward tied to audited quality and resolution. A cheaper unit price only helps when repeat contact and specialist rework remain healthy.

04 · SEQUENCE AND DECISIONS

I would scale the journeys that pass the review.

First 90 days

Audit automated resolution and recontact. Join issue records across channels. Establish the four-part scorecard and a weekly demand forecast. Choose two low-risk journeys for controlled pilots.

Year 1

Test product help, agent assistance, and partner routing against comparison groups. Add specialist escalation and incident playbooks before increasing volume.

Years 2–3

Expand the journeys that improve durable resolution, train backup partner capacity, and shift recurring issue clusters into Product and Engineering fixes.

Pause

A severe privacy, fraud, policy, or account-access error pauses the affected route and sends the issue to a human owner.

Investigate

Two weekly cohorts where recontact exceeds the matched comparison or accuracy trails it stop expansion while the team reviews cases.

Expand

Durable resolution improves while severe errors and backlog stay stable, so the team can add volume in stages.

My ask of leadership

Give Support Operations access to linked issue and outcome data, two pilot journeys, a flexible partner reserve, and a Product and Engineering review partner. I would return after 90 days with a measured staffing plan and a decision on each pilot.

APPENDIX · ASSUMPTIONS AND SOURCES

These are the assumptions I would test first.

Planning assumptions

The exercise supplies 1B weekly users, 150k weekly issues, and 60% automated resolution. My year-three case assumes 1.5× user growth, 1.25× more issues per user, and 1.3× human effort per case. A 75% verified automated rate is a conditional planning case.

At 60% automation, 281,250 × 40% × 1.3 gives 146,250 effort units. At 75%, 281,250 × 25% × 1.3 gives 91,406 units. A stress case with 65% automation and 1.5× human effort reaches about 148k units.

Seven-day repeat contact is a starting proxy for durable resolution. Longer issue types need a longer review window. I would validate issue mix, handling time, and current partner capacity before setting a hiring number.

External evidence

  • OpenAI's published support model connects customer surfaces, knowledge, evaluation, and frontline feedback.
  • COPC CX Standard informs customer operations and partner management.
  • ISO 18295-2 covers client oversight of outsourced and internal contact centres.
  • Generative AI at Work studied AI assistance for 5,179 support agents. I would measure local effects before using an efficiency gain in the forecast.
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework informs monitoring, human review, and recovery.

This is my independent recommendation for a hypothetical exercise. The scenarios use sample records and goals. I have no access to OpenAI internal support data.
