# Hiring-manager walkthrough

## First minute

Select Quality, Productivity, Capacity, or Cost to serve. Read the operating decision, then the specialist inputs and outputs in the chart.

Read Problem and Insight, then compare Actual, Goal, and Gap. Hover over a question mark or focus it with the keyboard to inspect each metric definition. The visible limit explains the evidence constraint.

Scan the action plan for the action, named owner, due date, and status box. Red means blocked or overdue, yellow means at risk, and green means on track. All dates and statuses describe the fictional October 15 review.

## Closer review

Expand supporting calculations and records to inspect arithmetic. Expand an action’s proof row to inspect completion evidence and links. The communication draft remains optional.

For Cost to serve, compare onshore cost with offshore cost after coaching and oversight. Review the performance gaps and conditional retention rule. Two passing checkpoints and a cost advantage support retention, while a failed or expired pilot requires a new commercial decision.

Pause the handoff pulse or use reduced-motion preferences. Ask Onigiri about metrics, gaps, action deadlines, uncertainty, or implementation limits.

The chart explains one deterministic engine’s proposed handoffs. It performs no external operating actions and introduces no personal career claims.
