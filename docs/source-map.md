# Source and assumption map

## Exercise baseline

The supplied take-home PDF sets a hypothetical starting point of 1 billion weekly active users, about 150,000 weekly support issues, and 60% automated resolution. It asks for a three-year outlook, strategic priorities, a future operating model, and measures of success. The exercise PDF stays outside this public repository.

## Planning assumptions

The recommendation uses illustrative Year 3 factors of 1.5× weekly users, 1.25× issues per user, and 1.3× human effort per case. The logic is that a larger user base raises demand, more products and business use cases can raise issue frequency, and automation can remove easier issues before a person handles the remaining queue. Year 1 and Year 2 step toward these factors. Verified automated resolution rises from the exercise's 60% starting point to 70%, 80%, and 88% in an illustrative stretch case. The 75% rate shows a downside case at Year 3 demand; it would leave about 70,000 weekly human cases and 91,000 effort units. None of these rates is an OpenAI target. The chart depicts automated resolution and human assistance; backlog remains outside its scope.

At Year 3 demand and 1.3× effort per human case, verified automation must exceed about 84% to lower modeled human effort below today's baseline. The 88% stretch case leaves about 34,000 weekly human cases and 44,000 effort units, a 27% reduction in effort. This path requires enough eligible issue volume, approved actions, durable resolution, and safe handoffs. It gives no credit for preventing issues through product fixes. A staffing number would require observed handle time by issue type, qualified hours, occupancy, shrinkage, and service windows. Released capacity would be redeployed first. Proposed pilots for invoice lookup and product how-to guidance require a Day 30 volume and risk review. The Day 90 targets of a 5-point durable-resolution gain, 10% lower human effort per eligible issue, and zero severe errors are illustrative gates, separate from OpenAI commitments.

It also supplies Glen Worthington's quoted description of the purpose of Support. The exercise supplies the hypothetical baseline, and my planning case supplies the multipliers.

## Albert's experience and the demonstration

Albert supplied four years of Meta BPO management across five onshore and offshore vendor partners, plus Meta scorecard measures for coverage, quality, workforce, sales funnel, and Ads AI adoption. The recommendation uses relevant measures inside four Support operating lenses and keeps business revenue as prioritization context. It makes no historical OpenAI Support performance claim.

The additional operating scenarios use fictional organizations, people, dates, goals, and records. One deterministic browser engine calculates their recommendations, and the visual agent chart replays proposed handoffs. The guide gives fixed answers from page content.
