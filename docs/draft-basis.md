## Draft

The five-page core take-home covers the assignment's four areas through the case for change, the three-year path, the plan with proof gates, and a financial decision. Diagnose, Design, Deliver appears as the method in the appendix. The strategic idea is an AI-led Support system that prevents repeat issues and resolves approved needs inside the product while people govern risk and improve the system. A stacked volume chart shows the Year 1–3 split between automated resolution and human assistance under the 500k-issue plan. The presentation describes the likely shift toward harder human cases through case counts and a qualitative capability view. The roadmap gives yearly AI resolution and human case targets with quality, backlog, and cost checks. Core 01 introduces the $118 million high-growth financial opportunity. Core 04 shows the three-year cost bridge and the $13.1 million downside if progress stalls. The appendix holds detailed assumptions, operating ownership, pilot gates, the full scorecard, financial notes, and a lower-demand sensitivity. The second tab remains a fictional demonstration.

## Omitted unsupported claims

The take-home leaves out historical Support service outcomes, OpenAI internal targets, and production multi-agent execution. Meta sales results remain separate from Support results.
