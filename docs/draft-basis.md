## Draft

The four-section take-home covers the case for change, the three-year path, strategic priorities, and the financial choice. The opening names the potential $114.1M high-case net saving and the three investments needed to pursue it. Core 03 orders Process, Product, and People by investment priority, with the purpose and action under each. The demand scenario combines weekly user growth with rising issue frequency and reaches 500,000 weekly issues. The annual roadmap targets 70%, 80%, and 90% checked AI resolution. The quarterly scorecard tracks Quality, Productivity, Capacity, and Cost to Serve across AI, partners, and specialists. Core 04 compares low, medium, and high financial cases on one demand path, then pairs leadership requests with the main risks and responses. The appendix keeps demand assumptions, the three-step issue flow with examples, financial inputs, and two sensitivity checks. The second tab remains a fictional demonstration.

## Omitted unsupported claims

The take-home leaves out historical Support service outcomes, OpenAI internal targets, and production multi-agent execution. Meta sales results remain separate from Support results.
