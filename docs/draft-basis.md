## Draft

The four-section take-home covers the case for change, the three-year path, strategic priorities, and opportunity size. The opening names the potential $114M high-case net saving and the three investments needed to pursue it. Core 03 splits an illustrative $20M across Process, Product, and People over three years. Process connects issue history and quality checks. Product funds approved AI actions and customer self-service. People funds partner training and embedded Support specialists at selected vendor sites. The demand scenario reaches 500,000 weekly issues, with automated resolution rising toward 90% and modeled vendor reps moving from about 940 today to 780 in Year 3. The quarterly scorecard tracks Quality, Productivity, Capacity, and Cost to Serve. Core 04 compares low, medium, and high financial cases on one demand path, then pairs leadership requests with the main risks and responses. The appendix keeps demand assumptions, operating examples, financial inputs, and sensitivity checks. The second tab remains a fictional demonstration.

## Omitted unsupported claims

The take-home leaves out historical Support service outcomes, OpenAI internal targets, and production multi-agent execution. Meta sales results remain separate from Support results.
